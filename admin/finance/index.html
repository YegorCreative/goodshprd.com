How these totals work

Revenue counts completed and paid sales before refunds, including tax and shipping in the order total. Collected counts successful payments. Gross profit is revenue minus recorded item costs and refunds. Estimated profit subtracts expenses as well. Missing costs make profit incomplete. Payment processing fees are included only when entered as expenses. Sales use their sale date; payments, refunds and expenses use their own dates. Currencies are never combined.
